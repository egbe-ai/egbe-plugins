# Security

Please report a suspected vulnerability in the Egbe plugin or its connector
privately, through this repository's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/egbe-ai/egbe-plugins/security/advisories/new),
or by email to support@egbe.ai with "Security" in the subject.

Do not open a public discussion, and do not include API keys, passwords, private
business records or full conversation transcripts. Describe the issue, the
affected plugin version (shown by `claude plugin list`) and the steps to
reproduce it. We will acknowledge your report and keep you informed while we
fix it.
