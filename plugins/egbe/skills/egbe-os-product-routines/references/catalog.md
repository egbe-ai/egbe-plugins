# Recurring operations

Use this catalogue to configure the caller's selected responsibility. Sources,
scope, authorization and the project method/plan determine what one run may do;
the entries do not prescribe which business priority or strategy to choose.

| Operation | Inputs and one-run output | Capabilities and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product health | Owned deployment, relevant errors/logs and safe journey checks; current availability and observed failures with evidence | errors, deploy; authorized repair through product-build. No real purchases or customer actions to manufacture a health check; platform faults can go to support. |
| Development/backlog | Accessible repository, tasks/PRs/CI and agreed scope; changed work, checks and blockers since the checkpoint | git for that repository's actual operations, product-build for authorized work. Verify PR/CI access, preserve work already in progress. |
| Advertising oversight | Owned campaigns, results and concrete test conditions; delivery/moderation/spend and reached conditions | Relevant channel skills and analytics. Observation does not authorize pause, optimization or additional spend. |
| Product measurements | Event definitions, comparable window/cohort and baseline; counts, source coverage and changes | analytics and payments; use the session evidence collector when reproducible event analysis is needed. |
| Customer feedback | Authorized feedback, reviews or replies; new records linked to product context | mail, git, analytics as relevant. Reading does not authorize sending replies; Egbe support tickets are platform support, not the product's customer-feedback store. |
| Acquisition-source updates | Agreed audience/topic/source scope and paid-tool allowance; new or changed source evidence since the checkpoint | search, scrape, apollo-lead-finder or organic-trend-watch as applicable. Finding an opportunity does not authorize posting, outreach or ads. |
| Email campaign operations | Known campaign/run, configured delivery protection and reply scope; new replies, delivery warnings, confirmed stops and current observations | instantly-campaign-launcher for replies, monitoring and optional bounded replenishment; Apollo for configured sourcing. Reuse its existing jobs. Factual snapshots do not decide that a test is finished or authorize another batch. |
| Business Loop | Agreed schedule/action scope, current project method and resumable work records; completed run, current checkpoint and delivery outcome | [Business Loop scheduling](product-reporting.md), domain skills for authorized work, product-report for its report. The method chooses the business focus; the scheduler does not grant new business authority. |
| Product digest | Requested mode, period, timezone and source evidence; verified brief or detailed report and delivery outcome | product-report and [reporting schedules](product-reporting.md). Preserve existing digest preferences; do not add a duplicate daily review alongside a Business Loop. |

Read the selected capability's current contract. Pass the current project method,
concrete plan and prior checkpoint to runs that interpret results; do not embed a
second strategy or a stale copy in the schedule. A bounded change check may use
previous evidence when its scope and freshness fit.
