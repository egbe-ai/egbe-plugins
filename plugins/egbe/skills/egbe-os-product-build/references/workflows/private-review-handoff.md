# Private review after handoff

Use this when the founder or another authorized reviewer needs to try the
customer journey or proposed paid result after implementation stops. Keep the
business, delivery format and implementation choices open. This workflow adds
no authority to spend, contact customers, accept agreements or expand access.

When the caller explicitly authorizes synthetic account testing, use that
permission within its named products and review window: generate fresh private
test credentials, create marked test accounts through normal signup, and
exercise the product's own non-binding test acknowledgments. Keep account and
terms-version observations in private test evidence and exclude synthetic
activity from real customer and revenue metrics. This does not authorize
changes to human accounts, third-party agreements or financial commitments.
Mandatory tool confirmations still govern credential operations and binding
agreements, even on an owner-controlled product. Do not relabel real terms or
switch tools to evade a required confirmation. A missing browser-policy
capability remains an explicit dependency, not permission supplied by this
workflow. Preserve actual Auth and consent proof as separate outcomes.

## Establish usable access

Before choosing the review method, establish the reviewer's available devices,
browser sign-ins, supported interactions and essential file capabilities. Account
for any required human action for credentials, consent or email verification.
Connected-service read access does not establish a signed-in browser session.
Use these facts in the existing plan and handoff; no separate intake form is
needed. Resolve routine choices within existing delegation.

Use normal account entry for Auth proof. After that proof, dependent work may
reuse Auth's private validation identity through the normal session authority.
A session created for testing does not prove signup, login or consent. A raw
cookie file is not a usable handoff when the reviewer's tools cannot load it.
Never introduce a public test-login route, copy an owner's browser cookies or
weaken authentication to make a review possible.

Keep the minimum authorized review access usable for the agreed review window,
within the caller's time and resource limits. Record its expiry and cleanup
owner; preserve the founder's ordinary access. When the reviewer can read the
shared project folder, a private Git-ignored file there, such as one under
`.local/`, is a supported credential handoff; keep it out of commits, public
pages and reports. Prefer expiry the product enforces itself, such as review
accounts or sessions it rejects after the deadline, so cleanup does not depend
on a process running after you stop. If an essential human step or
capability remains unavailable, state the precise gap and continue independent
authorized preparation. Do not report the missing check as passed.

## Leave a repeatable customer journey

Use the current serving product and the focused Auth, Payments and Analytics
workflows. Leave a supported way for the reviewer to repeat the relevant checks
with their actual access after the builder finishes. A written walkthrough,
local test output or old payment receipt alone does not provide that ability.

Payments owns validate-only requests and receipt verification. Repeat validation
to obtain fresh short-lived receipts when needed; do not extend receipt validity,
persist receipts as reusable credentials or create real charges as probes.
Keep no-charge checkout validation separate from signed lifecycle, durable
entitlement and delivery verification. Exercise those through supported isolated
synthetic checks; do not grant production paid access merely to claim a pass.

Trace the offer's complete applicable journey from entry and offer selection
through authenticated continuation, payment handling, access enforcement, the
actual promised result, saved-work recovery, support and measurement. Reuse
existing authoritative state rather than building parallel test logic. Check
account boundaries and duplicate processing where relevant. Label synthetic
identities and events and exclude them from customer and revenue claims.

For a file-based result, verify that the intended recipient can obtain, open,
use and recover the actual deliverable using the agreed tools. For a hosted
result, verify its corresponding return and recovery behavior. State narrower
coverage honestly; opening a preview is not proof of the full paid result.
Keep tests proportional to the actual offer rather than requiring every
possible account, payment or file feature.

## Carry the resource basis and remaining gaps

Use the accepted delivery, support and recovery obligations and the task's resource
basis. Read Wallet for current balances/rates when a resource check is needed.
Return a missing commercial decision to its owner instead of adapting the offer
inside technical review. Keep resource limits separate from unfinished authorized work.

Return the product location, repeatable review steps, necessary private access,
access lifetime, remaining human actions and verified/unverified outcomes in the
existing handoff. Keep secrets out of public plans and evidence. Completion of
this preparation does not authorize launch or establish demand or revenue.
