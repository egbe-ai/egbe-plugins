# Meta

Use this for feed-based paid acquisition where the audience may not be actively
searching for the offer. Combine it with the shared method; use `egbe-os-meta-ads`
for targeting/creative contracts, construction and operation records.

## Audience and creative hypothesis

Explain who should notice the ad while browsing and why the problem or benefit
matters now. A pain, desired outcome, identity or contrast can frame a creative
hypothesis; choose the framing for the audience rather than treating these as
a compulsory set of variants.

Make the visual and opening message communicate a relevant reason to stop
scrolling, then carry that promise into the destination. Adapt the composition
to the selected feed or story placement instead of assuming one crop fits all.

## Interpret response

A feed creative can earn reactions without intent to visit or buy. For a site
acquisition test, inspect the path from link response to visits and the intended
product action; do not optimize toward all-click engagement merely because it
looks stronger. Use the skill's metric definitions to keep these signals distinct.
