# Google Search

Use this when assessing or running a Search test that reaches people through
expressed search intent. It supplements the shared market and acquisition method; use
`egbe-os-google-ads` for API operations, platform constraints and campaign records.

## Demand and targeting

Use Google keyword ideas to investigate relevant demand. Compare query intent
with the actual offer, not just volume or a cheap click. Choose geography,
language, grouping and match types for the audience being tested, without a
fixed number of keywords, groups or variants.

For ambiguous shortlisted queries, reuse search-result evidence or check the
intended market's results when evidence is insufficient; do not research every
keyword separately. Keep material reasons for rejecting otherwise attractive
candidates in the existing plan.

Derive negative keywords from unwanted intent, not cost alone. Check their scope
and match types against wanted queries so they do not exclude the intended
audience. Explain the starting maximum CPC using available evidence and the
agreed spending limits; keep it distinct from expected average click cost.

## Ads and measurement

Prepare the full keyword selection, exclusions, groups, headlines, descriptions
and destinations. Check continuity from query intent to ad promise, landing
page and intended action.

Choose Google conversion actions that represent the intended outcome. Explain
separately whether they are for observation or bidding optimization: collecting
an action is not by itself a reason to let bidding optimize toward it.

## Interpreting a Search test

Where available, inspect actual search terms and their relevance, not only the
planned keyword list. Relate impressions and clicks to intent, spend and the
destination's outcomes. Check whether a mismatch comes from the searched intent,
match behavior, ad promise or landing experience before proposing a specific
keyword, exclusion, creative or destination change.
