# LinkedIn

Use this for audiences whose professional context matters to the offer. Combine
it with the shared method; use `egbe-os-linkedin-ads` for supported targeting,
formats, account access and operations.

## Reach the relevant buying situation

Connect professional role, organization and problem to the offer. Distinguish
the person responding from the buyer or decision-maker. Treat audience estimates
as estimates; if targeting is too narrow, explain the trade-off before broadening
it rather than silently sacrificing relevance for reach.

Design the message for the chosen format: a single-image ad can carry a clear
visual focal claim, while a small text-ad thumbnail cannot carry dense text.
Put the essential offer early enough to survive feed text truncation, and make
the next action fit the professional buying situation.

## Interpret the buying cycle

Evaluate lead quality and progression over the relevant buying window, not only
CTR or low-cost clicks. A useful qualified conversation may precede a purchase;
check whether it advances the intended organization's buying process, not merely
whether a professional profile submitted a form.
