# Prepare and run a test

Use this to prepare or continue a chosen test. The result is an agreed plan and
materials, execution that matches them, and an observable outcome with a real
follow-up arrangement.

## Develop the concrete plan

Create or update the working plan from the first substantive preparation, not
after launch. Reuse an existing plan; for new work use `plan.md` with the campaign,
or `egbe-docs/marketing/plans/<id>/plan.md` for cross-channel work. Link applicable
execution records rather than duplicating their technical state in the plan.
Keep proposed and approved choices distinguishable, with evidence, reasons and
remaining questions. The playbook is not a place for this test's targeting,
creative, spending amounts or schedule.

Make the question, audience/market, offer, desired action and comparison clear.
Carry forward applicable research and decisions; use [choose-test](choose-test.md)
only for unresolved choices. Budget, window and stop/checkpoint conditions must
fit the intended learning, available resources and uncertainty. State which
agreed limit ends the test first, and what remains inconclusive if it does.

Keep material launch and continuation conditions with their coverage in the
existing plan:
what is authorized, the limits and required safeguards, who acts, and what remains
unfulfilled. Make numeric conditions unambiguous where it changes the action:
what is counted, the denominator, scope, period/timezone, any minimum sample and
whether the boundary is inclusive. State the response to a trigger or missing/stale
required evidence. Link settings and verification records rather than copying
their technical state or creating a separate conditions document.

Identify the observed outcome and attribution: provider delivery/response and
the corresponding product action are different measurements. Reuse appropriate
events, links and tracking. Explain missing signals and what can still be learned
without them; do not make every test depend on purchase tracking. Agree on the
plan, measurement and any spending before preparing the launch.

## Prepare and review the visitor experience

Use any relevant channel method to prepare applicable targeting and exclusions,
materials and destinations. Explain material choices using evidence or labelled hypotheses.
Variants should test a meaningful difference; no fixed asset count is a method.
Ground claims in this product's capabilities and proof, not competitor copy.
Check the actual placement's crop, legibility and text display, not just a
full-size source image or an API payload.

Check continuity from audience intent through the promise and destination to
the intended action. If the destination needs unresolved experience decisions—what
it asks, shows, promises or hands off—use [Product experience](../product/experience.md)
with the test's agreed audience and offer. Carry the accepted design into implementation;
an unchanged accepted page or bounded technical repair does not need redesign.

Use `egbe-os-destination-readiness` for verification and
`egbe-os-campaign-landing-page` for agreed landing work. Inspect what customers can
actually do: an available page is not proof of a working form, payment or delivered
result. Record untested boundaries honestly. Propose missing product work
separately instead of implementing it incidentally under an acquisition request.

Use [working together](../product/collaboration.md) for this material review.

## Arrange observation

Establish who will check outcomes and act on stop conditions. Choose review
timing from the learning window, reporting delay, pending moderation and spending
exposure; account for signal delay and check frequency when describing how quickly
an action can follow. Use an existing routine or `egbe-os-product-routines` for agreed
scheduling; verify it is active and its action scope is understood. A written
future check is not automation.

Before launch, match each required safeguard to an arrangement that actually
observes its condition and performs the agreed response. When continuing or
resuming a test, recover the verified state of its ongoing protection. Reuse valid
verification; a one-time preparation check does not become a permanent gate.
A readable metric, accepted configuration, registered schedule or active campaign
alone does not establish that required monitoring works.

For an unmet or unverified required condition, explain its consequence for the
next eligible action and follow the agreed response within current authority.
Obtain the missing evidence or agree a workable alternative; do not silently
change a limit or measurement definition to fit the tool. An alternative must
fit the actual availability and capabilities of whoever will perform it.
If neither is feasible, explain why the dependent step is not ready.
Missing proof is not proof that protection is absent or a threshold has failed.
Notify the founder of monitoring failures and their effect on current work.

## Build, verify and launch

Confirm the available execution route and who will operate it. Use the relevant
skill for construction, technical validation, ownership, retry and recovery;
research capability alone does not imply automated publishing. If execution needs
the founder or another authorized operator, make that handoff explicit.
Never create real campaigns or other provider objects just to test an API's
request format. Use documentation, local validation or an
explicit provider validation facility; a real paused campaign is still an object.

Inspect the agreed materials and setup before launch. Resolve or report differences
rather than changing the offer, audience or budget just to pass a check. Show readiness,
review/measurement gaps, applicable spending settings and the observation/stop
arrangement; get approval before enabling delivery or publishing.

Bind observations to the actual run, its period and agreed plan/terms. If a
provider campaign is reused for a changed test, keep those runs distinguishable
in the existing records instead of pooling their outcomes.

Report actual state: created or published does not mean seen; for ads, enabled,
eligible, awaiting review and receiving traffic are different facts. Keep confirmed
identifiers and unfinished operations in the execution records; factual launch
and remaining checks belong in results.
Missing data is not zero, and successful creation is not customer acquisition.

At the relevant evidence or decision point, continue with [evaluate](evaluate.md).
