# Work with the founder

By default, present meaningful intermediate results and get approval before the
next dependent step. Show the recommendation, why it fits, material uncertainty
and the decision needed. Review related materials together, not every tool call;
reuse unchanged approvals with their scope. Do not save every decision for one
final approval or ask again to perform an already agreed bounded repair.

Show complete proposed materials and behavior, not just illustrative fragments.
Use `egbe-os-report-ui` when a visual comparison helps; derive it from the
working plan and keep it alongside the materials, not as another decision source.
Follow EGBE for authority, communication and resumable handoff. Recommendations
and prepared materials are not executed changes.

Keep the scope of a method adaptation explicit. A preference only for advertising
belongs in that domain's method; change this shared guidance only when the agreed
way of working applies across domains.
