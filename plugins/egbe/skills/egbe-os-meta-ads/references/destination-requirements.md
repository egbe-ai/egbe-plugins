# Meta destination requirements

Use only for Meta-linked destinations. Reuse the common journey evidence rather
than running the account, targeting or tracking readiness workflow.

For a complete visitor-path review, apply the `egbe-os-destination-readiness` skill with
this reference in one audit run. If missing locally, its full bundle is available
at `GET $OS_URL/skills/destination-readiness`.

[Meta Advertising Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/)
were checked 2026-09-29. Read the relevant current section when making a policy
finding; Meta's review includes associated landing pages and other destinations.

- **Relevance:** compare the advertised company/product and offer with what the
  landing page actually offers. An unrelated product or misleading brand handoff
  needs a correction or visible relationship explanation, not just a working URL.
- **Prohibited commercial practices:** inspect consequential destination claims,
  concealed commitments and apparent impersonation. Explain the observed deception
  or unsupported promise; a different disclosed parent brand is not enough.
- **Cybersecurity:** flag an observed phishing or malicious destination. Do not
  download or execute suspicious content to verify it.
- **Offer-specific destination restrictions:** if inspected content raises a
  restricted-category concern, open that category's section in the Standards
  and apply only its destination/content obligations. Do not turn this into
  account eligibility, campaign targeting or a general legal audit.

Report the affected Meta path, source section and concrete repair/recheck.
Separate confirmed visitor problems, evidenced policy risks and unresolved facts.
Old policy-page slugs or generic “all sites need these documents” claims are not
a substitute for the current applicable rule. Provider review remains separate.
