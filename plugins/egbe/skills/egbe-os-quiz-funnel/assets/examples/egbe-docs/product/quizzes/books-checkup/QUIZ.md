## Summary

- **Product:** Done-for-you bookkeeping for freelancers and small studios: a bookkeeper reconciles the accounts every month and catches up missed months.
- **Application:** https://example-books.egbe.app (repository `example-books`)
- **Segment:** Freelancers behind on their books — Self-employed for more than a year, invoices clients directly, and has a tax filing due within three months. Problem: Months of unreconciled income and expenses make the filing slow and stressful.
- **Offer:** Books caught up and ready for filing in 14 days. Mechanism: One bookkeeper reconciles every missed month from bank exports; the client only answers questions. Terms: Free 20-minute books review call. Catch-up from $290, then $95 per month.
- **Goal:** call — booking page https://cal.example.com/books-review
- **Route:** `/quiz/books-checkup`
- **Language:** en
- **Traffic:** meta_ads in US; arriving promise: Find out what your books need before tax season.
- **Consent:** no planned geography is on the consent list
- **Length:** 6 questions, 2 interstitial screens, 5 results

The offer is not for:

- Businesses whose books are up to date and already kept in an accounting app

Claims to avoid:

- guaranteed tax savings
- protection from an audit
