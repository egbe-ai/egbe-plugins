# Brief founder update

Give the founder a useful picture in one short, scannable message. Cover the
areas below when applicable, combining them rather than making a long checklist:

- Available Egbe funds and any material spending or funding constraint.
- Product availability and confirmed consequential failures.
- Connected advertising: what is running, blocked or stopped; spend and
  remaining agreed budget where known.
- Today's visitors, registrations, meaningful activation and purchases/revenue,
  distinguishing unavailable measurements from zero.
- Important changes since the previous comparable report.

Put an urgent problem first. Separate status, results and the next step with
short headings or bold labels; use bullets for distinct items, not a wall of
paragraphs. Explain status in founder language rather than API codes, skill
names or internal IDs. Name the outcome Egbe enables, not the instruction file.
Name the reporting period and surface coverage gaps that affect a decision.
For an empty project, state what is actually available and the product's current
stage; do not fabricate a funnel or suggest repairing nonexistent instrumentation.
Combine areas with the same status. Prefer a precise label and scope to a
paragraph of qualifications; keep collection details in the snapshot.
Give each bullet one main message. Omit explanations of distinctions already
clear from the label, and empty integrations that do not affect today's decision.

End with the next step justified by the current project method and plan: the
problem or opportunity, proposed improvement, purpose and what I can do with Egbe.
Keep an agreed continuation as the next step when that is the conclusion; do not
invent novelty to fill the format. Preserve uncertainty and any decision needed.

A scheduled digest is still delivered when nothing changed, proportionately
short; an alerts-only routine follows its agreed notification condition instead.
Preserve any delivered message as `summary.md`.
Do not create `report.md` and HTML just to expand a brief update.
